# Ethics, Privacy, and Responsible AI Use

**Project:** AI Portfolio Website Content Creator  
**Student:** Venkatakrishnan  
**Department:** B.Tech Information Technology, 2nd Year

## 1. Introduction

AI tools can help with writing, coding, planning, and reviewing a portfolio website. However, AI-generated content may be inaccurate, biased, repetitive, or unsuitable for the intended audience. Human review is necessary before using it.

## 2. Accuracy and Honesty

- Verify factual claims before publishing.
- Do not invent qualifications, certifications, experience, projects, or achievements.
- Describe technical skills according to actual knowledge and experience.
- Report testing results and feedback honestly.
- Correct misleading or inaccurate AI-generated content.

## 3. Privacy and Data Protection

- Never include passwords, authentication tokens, or private account information in AI prompts.
- Share only the personal information necessary for the portfolio.
- Obtain permission before publishing another person's personal information, comments, or photographs.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary visitor information.
- Review contact-form behavior to understand what information is included in messages.

## 4. Copyright and Attribution

- Check the usage terms for images, icons, fonts, and other assets.
- Prefer original work or assets with suitable licenses.
- Provide attribution where required by the applicable license.
- Do not copy another student's portfolio and present their original work as your own.
- Review AI-generated content before publication and follow institutional rules about disclosing AI assistance.

## 5. Bias and Accessibility

- Use respectful and inclusive language.
- Avoid assumptions about people based on personal characteristics.
- Use readable text, sufficient color contrast, descriptive image alternatives, and clear navigation.
- Test the website at different screen sizes.
- Ensure that interactive controls can be understood and used accessibly.

## 6. Transparency

AI assistance may be used for brainstorming, drafting, code suggestions, and proofreading. The student remains responsible for reviewing, adapting, and understanding the submitted work. Any disclosure required by the college must be followed.

## 7. Limitations of AI

AI-generated output can contain errors, outdated information, insecure code, or recommendations that do not fit the project. AI tools cannot replace actual browser testing, human judgment, or verification of project claims.

## 8. Risk and Mitigation Table

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Incorrect AI-generated information | Verify claims before publishing |
| Generic or repetitive content | Edit and personalize the text |
| Exposure of private information | Avoid sharing sensitive data |
| Copyright or licensing problems | Check asset permissions and attribution |
| Inaccessible design | Review contrast, navigation, and text alternatives |
| Website bugs | Test the site and correct identified problems |
| Unsupported claims about results | Report only work and evidence that actually exist |

## 9. Conclusion

Responsible AI use requires accuracy, privacy protection, respect for intellectual property, accessibility, and human oversight. These principles should guide the development, testing, documentation, and submission of the portfolio.
